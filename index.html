Our manifesto

Glory to the cup that waits for you. Beans chosen with obsession, roasted with patience, poured with intention. All that is left to do is slow down and taste.

AVANA started with one stubborn idea: a small town deserves a world class cup. Every drink on our bar is measured, timed and tasted before it reaches your hands.
